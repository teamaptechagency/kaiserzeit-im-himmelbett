# Reviewing the KAISERZEIT website preview

A short guide to the preview link — what to look at, how to leave feedback,
and how to put your own photos and wording in.

Nothing here can break anything. Every change is saved separately from the
design and can be undone.

---

## Your link

**Preview:** `https://<preview-url>/`
**Key for making changes:** `<key>`

The link opens the site exactly as a guest would see it. Click through all
seven pages, and try it on your phone as well as a computer — the layout
adapts, and it is worth seeing both.

Anyone you send this link to sees a normal website. They cannot change
anything, and they cannot see the comments.

## Two things we need you to decide

Both were left in the preview deliberately so you can see them side by side
rather than choose from a description.

**1. The search results on the home page.** In the booking bar there are two
small buttons, *Dropdown* and *Popup*. Click each one, then click *Check
Availability*. Dropdown opens the apartment list underneath the bar; Popup
opens it in a window over the page. Tell us which you prefer.

**2. The apartments page layout.** It currently offers two versions — the four
apartments grouped into two property cards, or shown as four separate cards.
Let us know which one to keep.

## Leaving comments — the main thing

This is the most useful thing you can do, and the quickest.

1. Click **Comment** in the bottom-left corner.
2. Enter the key the first time. Your browser remembers it afterwards.
3. Click anywhere on the page — next to a heading, on a photo, in the margin.
4. Type your note and press **Post**.

A numbered pin stays where you clicked. We reply underneath it, and mark it
*Resolve* once it is done. The number on the toolbar shows how many are still
open.

Pins stay visible the whole time you are signed in, whichever tool you are
using, and they are still there when you come back another day. Nothing you do
here is kept only in your browser — photos, wording, backgrounds and comments
are all stored on the server, so they survive closing the tab, signing out, or
opening the link on a different device.

Please comment freely — "this photo is too dark", "this text should be
bigger", "wrong address here". A note pinned to the exact spot is far clearer
than an email describing it, and nothing gets lost.

## Putting your own photos in

The design uses placeholder boxes wherever a photo is needed. There are 46 of
them: apartment galleries, headers, the 360° panoramas, the property cards.

**Drag a photo straight onto any placeholder.** It uploads and appears
immediately, for everyone with the link.

To see what is still missing, click **All images** on the toolbar. That page
lists every photo the site needs, grouped by apartment, with a bar showing how
many are done. It is the easiest way to work through them.

Photos can be replaced as often as you like — just drop a new one on top.

## Changing the wording

Double-click any text, type, and press **Enter**.

The German and English versions are separate. Changing a German heading does
not change the English one — switch language using the **DE / EN** control in
the navigation and edit the other version too.

To undo, set the text back to what it was.

---

## Sizing a photo

Hover a photo you have added and a small row of controls appears on it:

- **Cover / Fit** — fill the space and crop, or show the whole photo
- **↑ / ↓** — when it is cropped, which part to keep
- **Slider** — zoom in, up to three times

These are remembered per photo, so the same picture looks the same everywhere
it appears. Put them all back to the start and the photo returns to the
original setting.

## Changing a section's background

Switch to **Background** on the toolbar and click any section. You can set a
colour, lay a tint over it — dark, light, or any colour you pick — and adjust
how strong the tint is. You can also make a photo more or less transparent, or
drop in a background image.

**Text colour** sits in the same panel. If you make a light section dark, set
it to **Light** and the writing in that section switches to the pale colours
used elsewhere on dark backgrounds — headings, body text and the gold accents
all adjust together, so it still looks like the same design. **Dark** does the
reverse. **Auto** leaves it as designed.

**Reset** puts the whole section back to the original design.

## Changing the typeface and the corners

**Style** on the toolbar holds two things that apply to the whole site at once.

**Typeface** — three pairings:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Original** | Playfair Display + EB Garamond — as designed |
| **Modern** | Cormorant Garamond + Jost |
| **Classic** | Libre Baskerville + Lato |

**Corners** — how rounded things are, in three groups: **Buttons**, **Cards**
and **Fields**. Move a slider and everything in that group changes together,
so the site stays consistent. Round avatars stay round.

**Reset** puts the corners back exactly as designed, and Original does the
same for the typeface — so try anything you like.

## A few things to know

**Links do not work while you are signed in for editing.** This is on purpose
— the link text is editable, so a single click would otherwise take you to
another page mid-sentence. Use the page dropdown on the toolbar to move
around. Click **Stop editing** to browse normally again.

**Booking is not real yet.** You can pick dates, see a price and reach a
"Booking Confirmed" screen, but nothing is stored and no payment is taken.
Sign-in and availability are placeholders too. Those are part of the real
build, not this preview.

**This is a preview, not the finished website.** It exists so the design, the
wording and the photos can be settled before the real site is built. The legal
pages, cookie settings and the live booking system come later.

## If something looks wrong

Leave a comment on the spot — that is always the fastest route, and we see it
immediately. If the page itself misbehaves, a reload usually clears it; your
changes are already saved.
